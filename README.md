# AI Lead Finder Email Generator

A lightweight, standalone browser tool for turning lead research into personalized sales emails.

It runs entirely in the browser with no dependencies, account, or external service required.

## Open it

Double-click `index.html`, or right-click it and choose **Open With > Safari/Chrome**.

No installation, server, API key, or internet connection is required. All lead data stays in the browser tab and is not saved automatically.

## Files

- `index.html` - page structure
- `styles.css` - visual design and responsive layout
- `app.js` - live email generation, copy, and clear controls

## Use it with the Lead Finder

Copy business, contact, location, and research details from the Lead Finder into the matching fields. Add your offer and proof point, choose a tone and CTA, then copy the generated email.

## Privacy

The app does not transmit or persist entered data. Everything remains in the current browser tab and is cleared when the page is refreshed or closed.
